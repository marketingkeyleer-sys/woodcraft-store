    How a custom order works

    1. 01

      Choose & customise

      Pick a design, then its wood, size and options. The 3D preview updates with every choice.

    2. 02

      Send it on WhatsApp

      Your order arrives in our chat with every detail. No sign-up and no payment on the website.

    3. 03

      We confirm the details

      We check size, wood, price and delivery date with you, and start work only after you say yes.

    4. 04

      Made & delivered

      Cut, shaped, oiled and packed by hand, then shipped to your door.
